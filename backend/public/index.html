— Our Purpose

Every weave, every brushstroke,
every carving traceable
to a Bhutanese hand.

Labzo is built to carry the Zorig Chusum the thirteen traditional arts of Bhutan — into the hands of the world, without losing the hands that make them.

Direct from the workshop

No middle broker. Every artisan sets their own price and ships from their own studio — in Thimphu, Paro, Bumthang, or beyond.

Craft you can trust

Every seller is visited before their first listing goes live. We verify the craft, not just the paper.

Fair to the maker

Labzo takes a flat 8% — far below the global average. The rest returns to the artisan, their family, and their workshop.

— Our Values

Gross National Happiness

We measure success in the wellbeing of makers, not just the volume of sales.

Zorig Chusum

Preserving Bhutan's thirteen traditional arts from thag-zo weaving to shing-zo woodwork.

Livelihood

We aim to provide fianical independence to all artist.
